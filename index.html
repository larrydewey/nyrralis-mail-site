Nyrralis Mail

A private email client for Android. It runs entirely on your phone and talks only to the mail servers of the accounts you add.

What it does

  • One inbox for Gmail, Google Workspace, Outlook.com, Microsoft 365, JMAP servers such as Fastmail, and any IMAP/SMTP server.
  • New mail arrives over a direct connection to your mail server, without Google Play services or Firebase.
  • Remote images and tracking pixels are blocked until you allow them.
  • Messages are stored in an encrypted database on the device, and the app can be locked with your fingerprint or device PIN.
  • Optional OpenPGP encryption and signing.

Signing in with Google or Microsoft

For Gmail and Microsoft accounts the app uses OAuth: you sign in on Google's or Microsoft's own page in your browser, and the app receives a token that lets it read and send your mail over IMAP, SMTP or Microsoft Graph. Your password is never given to the app. You can remove its access at any time in your Google account or Microsoft account settings.

Who makes it

Nyrralis Mail is a personal project by Larry Dewey. It is not distributed through Google Play and has no server of its own. Questions: open an issue.
